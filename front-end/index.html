What is impact investing?

Impact Investing is being called the New Philanthropy, the New Investing, the New Corporate HR and the new Family Legacy Planning.

Instead of giving away money that depletes a philanthropist's capital to make a difference, or investing in companies whose only purpose is to provide financial ROI, impact investors invest to advance their social and financial goals, but also their personal goals (whether corporate or family goals).

Impact Investors lend money to nonprofit organizations or for-profit companies whose products or services advance causes, they buy equity in those for-profits, and they share in revenue of profitable social projects of both nonprofits and for-profits.
